## 16. Category-aware semantic interpretation

Business category/domain context should be used as an **interpretation prior only**, not evidence.

Example:

```text
review:
“La limpieza es impecable”

business category:
dental clinic

interpretation:
facility cleanliness / praise
NOT dental cleaning
```

versus:

```text
“Conseguí una cita para una limpieza al día siguiente”

business category:
dental clinic

interpretation:
limpieza dental
provider = focal business
event = performed
```

Target semantic proposition fields include:

```text
normalized offering
exact evidence quote
provider_role
event_type
stance
sentiment
branch_scope
temporal_status
```

Suggested provider roles:

```text
FOCAL_BUSINESS
EXTERNAL_PROVIDER
REVIEWER
UNKNOWN
```

Suggested event types:

```text
PERFORMED
CURRENTLY_OFFERED
SCHEDULED
RECOMMENDED
DISCUSSED
DECLINED
REFERRED_ELSEWHERE
HYPOTHETICAL
HISTORICAL
UNKNOWN
```

LLM interprets; deterministic validators still decide whether output is grounded and usable.
