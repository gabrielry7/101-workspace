# 16. Important principles learned

## Missing evidence is not negative evidence

```text
no website observed
≠ business has no website

no contact captured
≠ low-fit business

no service evidence
≠ business does not offer service

provider request failed
≠ business state is negative
```

## Source and subject attribution matter

```text
directory vendor email
≠ business email

UK dentist recommends implant
≠ focal clinic offers implant

"clinic was clean"
≠ dental cleaning service
```

## Category is context, not evidence

Business category can help a semantic interpreter understand language.

It cannot itself establish an offering.

## Branch scope matters

Location-specific evidence cannot silently become business-wide or another branch's fact.

## LLMs should interpret evidence, not become truth

```text
full review
+ business/category context
        ↓
LLM semantic interpretation
        ↓
typed proposition
        ↓
exact quote/source validation
        ↓
Review Intelligence
        ↓
Offering Intelligence
        ↓
Truth / owner confirmation policy
```
