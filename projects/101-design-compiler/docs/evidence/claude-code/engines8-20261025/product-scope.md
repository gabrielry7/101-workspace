# Scope: eight restaurant style engines

101 Design Compiler renders validated business facts through shared Astro components. This task expands its existing restaurant laboratory from four to eight style operators: keep the four preliminarily liked directions, add two general-purpose and two artistic choices, then stop visual expansion and document the next integration work.

Visitors are Spanish-speaking restaurant guests scanning an ordinary menu, choosing among forty dishes and opening a dedicated detail page for ingredients, allergens, synthetic calories, price and optional illustrative media. They may explore a demonstration selection or reservation, read the story, visit information, journal, opinions and contact. Every commercial function and business fact is clearly fictional until connected to authorized real data.

The user has delegated the choice of artistic concepts to Claude and asks for concrete results rather than additional approval rounds. Existing approved-looking directions remain intact. New choices must differ structurally, retain mobile and keyboard usability, and work with missing media. No data inferred from stock photography, no real booking/payment promises, no new framework, generator or backend. Codex owns requirements, architecture, review, QA and integration; real Claude CLI implements frontend design in an isolated worktree. No automatic merge or push.

Success: eight selectable registered operators, complete forty-dish flow for each, unchanged current four outputs, licensed local multimedia and an actionable roadmap to upstream data, capabilities, generation/review and a measured pilot. This is preliminary restaurant lab work, not production acceptance or universal multivertical validation.
